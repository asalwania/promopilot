# Mechanisms are compared on the candidate set's expected numbers, by value, with the plan line standing for its own mechanism

E7 (#38) adds the mechanism comparator (SPEC F-02, §7.3 `mechanisms.compare(sku, region, context)`), the `compare_mechanisms` tool and a comparison on every plan line of the session read model. Issue #38 and spec #9 say which mechanisms are compared (BUNDLE only with a complement), which numbers each outcome carries, and that each mechanism is shown at its best depth, duration and segment within the request's constraints. They do not say where the numbers come from, what "best" means, how the plan line's own mechanism is shown, what happens to a mechanism with no admissible option, which options count as "within the constraints", or what the tool takes. We chose these with the owner (decisions D1–D6 on #38):

- **D1. The numbers are the demand model's expected values, taken from a generated candidate set (ADR 0035).** This departs from F-02 AC1's wording ("a comparison table … from the simulator"). The Monte Carlo simulator (#39) is built in parallel, and the candidate set holds the numbers the optimiser chose from, so the comparison and the plan agree to the rupee. P10–P90 bands from the simulator can be added to each outcome after #39 lands, without changing how the best option is picked.
  - We rejected simulator P50s, which would block #38 on #39.
- **D2. "Best" is the highest value**: incremental profit − cannibalisation + halo + clearance value. That is what the optimiser counts per option (ADR 0036). Mechanisms are ranked by the same value, highest first; ties keep the order PCT_OFF, BOGO, BUNDLE, FIXED_PRICE.
  - We rejected incremental profit alone. It would rank a mechanism the optimiser picked for clearance value or halo below one it rightly passed over.
- **D3. The plan line stands for its own mechanism.** `compare(..., chosen=line)` shows that mechanism with the plan line's own option, marked `chosen`, even when budget, margin or a pairwise term pushed the optimiser off that mechanism's best option. Every other mechanism shows its best option. A `chosen` line for another SKU or region, or not among the options, is a `ValueError`.
  - We rejected showing each mechanism's best option with only a flag for whether it equals the plan line. The manager would then not see the chosen option's numbers next to the alternatives.
- **D4. A mechanism whose every option broke a per-line rule is listed last, with no numbers and the rules it broke.** Option generation now records, per anchor SKU, region and mechanism, every `PruneReason` it pruned options for (`PromoOptions.pruned_by_mechanism`). The comparator lists them in rule order.
  - `duplicate_price` is never listed. A shallower FIXED_PRICE depth offered that charm price and was pruned for its own reason.
  - `PruneReason` moves to `promopilot.domain` so plan revisions can carry it. `promopilot.optimizer` re-exports it.
  - The common case is BOGO on a SKU that is not overstocked: 50% off sells below unit cost at every category margin (ADR 0037), so it shows `below_cost`.
  - We rejected leaving such mechanisms out, which hides why they are missing. We also rejected predicting them anyway, which goes beyond "within the request's constraints" and costs another prediction batch.
- **D5. "Within the request's constraints" means every option the candidate set kept**: inside the promo window, no deeper than the maximum discount, not below unit cost unless overstocked, P90 units within available stock (a BUNDLE partner's too). Options with negative value are shown as they are. The marketing budget and the minimum margin stay plan-level (ADR 0007), so no option is dropped for its promo cost or margin alone.
  - We rejected also dropping options whose promo cost alone exceeds the budget, and keeping only options the optimiser may select (worth at least a paisa). The second would show every mechanism that loses money as unavailable.
- **D6. `compare_mechanisms` takes a planning request, a SKU id and a region.** It generates only that SKU and region's options, the way `generate_candidates` does for the whole scope, then compares them. It stands alone, so E8's planner can call it before, or without, a candidate set. SPEC §9.6 lists it before `generate_candidates`.
  - The request must be for the tool's bound as-of week (ADR 0032). A region outside the request's scope, a SKU outside it or unknown to the data, and a category the data lacks are `invalid_input`. A missing model is `model_unavailable` and missing inventory is `data_unavailable`, as for `generate_candidates`. Both tools now share `load_option_context`.
  - We rejected taking a `candidate_set_id`. It is faster, but it needs `generate_candidates` first and fails once the set has been dropped from the store.
- **One BUNDLE row**, not one per complement: the best-value option over every detected complement as partner. Spec #9 asks for one outcome per applicable mechanism. A BUNDLE outcome names its anchor and partner SKUs, the pair's basket lift and its expected incremental profit (F-05 AC3). BUNDLE is compared only when the SKU has a detected complement (F-02 AC2). A BUNDLE option whose partner the relations no longer list as a complement is ignored.
- **Effective prices come from `promopilot.economics`** (ADR 0005, ADR 0015): PCT_OFF at the depth off, BOGO at 50% off, FIXED_PRICE at the charm price ending in 9, and BUNDLE at the depth off each SKU's own base price. That is the pair's discount split pro-rata by base price, so the two prices add up to the pair's price at the depth off.
- **The domain types** are `MechanismOption` (the option, anchor and partner SKU, basket lift, effective prices, units, revenue, gross profit, margin, promo cost, incremental profit, cannibalisation, halo, clearance value, value) and `MechanismOutcome` (mechanism, best option or None, chosen, unavailable reasons). `PlanRevisionLine.mechanism_comparison` is a tuple of outcomes, empty for revisions planned before E7.
- **The session compares every plan line on the options it already generated.** It does not predict anything again. Migration 0005 adds a nullable JSONB column `plan_lines.mechanism_comparison`. Lines stored before it read back with an empty comparison. OpenAPI, the frontend types and the zod schemas follow. The mechanism drawer that shows the comparison is E10's.

## Consequences

- On the seed-42 demo brief (weeks 108–109, ₹2 lakh, ADR 0037), the 35 plan lines are compared in about 0.3 s in all, on the options already generated.
  - The lines use 18 FIXED_PRICE and 17 PCT_OFF.
  - On 33 lines the chosen mechanism ranks first. On the other two, the best option of another mechanism (PCT_OFF on one, BUNDLE on the other) is worth a little more alone, and the optimiser passed it over for a plan-level reason.
  - 22 lines have a detected complement, and every one of them has an admissible BUNDLE.
  - BOGO is unavailable (`below_cost`) on 21 lines. On the other 14, the SKU is overstocked and BOGO is compared with its numbers. On the lines inspected, its incremental profit was negative, and clearance value offset part or all of that loss.
  - One `compare_mechanisms` call, which generates its own SKU and region's options, takes about 0.9 s.
- A comparison can show a mechanism, or a depth of the chosen mechanism, worth more alone than the plan line. The optimiser left it out for a plan-level reason (budget, margin, a promoted-SKU cap or a pairwise term), which the line's `why_chosen` (`best_for_sku_region`) and the revision's binding constraints explain.
- Migration 0005 may collide with other E7 tickets adding a migration on top of 0004. Whichever merges second renumbers its migration and points it at the first.
